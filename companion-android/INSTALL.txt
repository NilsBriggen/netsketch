NetSketch Survey — the companion app for Android phones (Android 10 or newer)

Install
  1. Copy NetSketch-Survey.apk to your phone (USB cable, cloud drive, or e-mail it to yourself).
  2. Open it on the phone. Android asks to allow installing apps from this source — allow it once.
  3. Open "NetSketch Survey". The first measurement asks for permission to see nearby Wi-Fi networks
     (Android files this under location), and Location has to be switched on while you measure.

Link it to NetSketch (recommended)
  1. On the computer: overview → Real measurements → "Link a phone (live)". A 4-digit code appears.
  2. On the phone: tap "Link to NetSketch" (top right). It finds the computer on your Wi-Fi by itself;
     if not, type the address NetSketch shows. Enter the code.
  3. Your floor plan appears on the phone. Pick the storey, tap where you're standing — the room name
     is filled in for you — and press "Measure here". The point appears in NetSketch right away,
     pinned exactly where you tapped.
  If the phone can't connect, the computer's firewall is blocking it: allow ports 47878 (TCP) and
  47879 (UDP). Points measured while not linked stay on the phone and are sent as soon as it's linked.

Without a computer nearby
  Measure anyway (it asks for the storey instead), then "Save & share file" and import the file in
  NetSketch under Real measurements → Import measurements…, and drag each pin to where you stood.

In NetSketch afterwards
  In the list of measured networks, networks named like yours are linked to your devices. For the
  others choose "Add as a neighbour" (or "Add … neighbours" for all): they get their measured
  channels, widths and busy time, placed where their signal fits your readings. Drag one to where it
  really is and press "Fit to measurements" to refine.

Build it yourself: ./build.sh (needs a JDK and the Android SDK; no Gradle).
